Executive Summary

Following version upgrades deployed between 28 April – 8 May 2026, API response performance during working hours improved significantly. The improvement is consistent and measurable, confirming the effectiveness of the recent platform upgrades.

06 Time-slot analysis & recommendations

06:00 – 08:59  ·  Early morning

Most stable slot. Avg improved from 90ms to 76ms post-upgrade. Single outlier on 05 May (07:00, 353ms) was isolated and not repeated. No slow events in this window after upgrade.

09:00 – 11:59  ·  Morning peak — watch closely

Only slot where post-upgrade avg is slightly higher (+7% at 09h, +23% at 10h). This is driven by the 13 May incident (310ms avg, 16 slow events). Structurally improved but requires continued monitoring.

12:00 – 17:59  ·  Afternoon — significant gain

P95 at 15:00 dropped from 5,088ms to 81ms after upgrade — the largest single improvement in the dataset. Previously the riskiest slot; now among the most stable.

18:00 – 23:30  ·  Evening — consistently clean

Avg improved from 108–124ms to 77–82ms across all evening hours. Zero slow events post-upgrade. This block now serves as the performance baseline benchmark.
